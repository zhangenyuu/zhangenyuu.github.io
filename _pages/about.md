I am a mathematics student at the University of Waterloo, with interests in probability theory, stochastic processes, and their applications to quantitative finance. I am particularly interested in martingale theory, stopping times, stochastic calculus, and stochastic control.

Previously, I worked as a quant at Squarepoint Capital and Mackenzie Investments, focusing on quantitative modeling, statistical research, and systematic trading strategies.
I will be joining a research team in New York as a Quantitative Research Analyst in April 2027.
