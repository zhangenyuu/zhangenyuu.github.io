--- layout: archive title: "Selected Course Notes" permalink: /notes/ author_profile: true --- These are my personal lecture notes from some of my favourite courses at the University of Waterloo. Written alongside the lectures, the notes aim to provide a self-contained and rigorous treatment of the course material, including definitions, theorems, proofs, and detailed mathematical derivations. They are intended to serve as comprehensive resources for learning and reviewing the material independently. I am considerably better at writing mathematics than typing it, so the notes almost surely contain typos. Fortunately, these appear to have negligible effects on convergence to the intended meaning.
